A fictional camera bought with a fictional card at a fictional store. helloACT would derive the three cards below from the receipt the owner holds, the manufacturer's published terms and the card's published benefits — each with its source and date.

Manufacturer warranty

2 years · ends March 2027

From the maker's published terms for this model (example). Covers defects in materials and workmanship. Registration: not on file — one of the gaps helloACT would name.

LIKELY · CHECK RECOMMENDED
Card benefit

+1 year extended warranty

The example card's published benefit guide extends the maker's warranty by up to one year. "Likely" because the benefit depends on the card that paid — a fact the owner attests, not one helloACT can verify.

GAP
Accidental damage

Not covered — by either

Neither the warranty nor the card benefit covers drops, spills or theft. This is the kind of gap helloACT would surface — and, one day, help you close.
